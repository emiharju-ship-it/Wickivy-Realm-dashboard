    CONTINENTAL LAWS

    Rule 1: Excommunicado

    Players declared Excommunicado forfeit all protections. Any assassin may engage them anywhere except Concierge designated safe zones.

    Rule 2: Temporary Sanctuary

    Remaining within protection radius of the Continental (Town Hall) grants absolute sanctuary. Leaving radius declares re-entry into combat field.

    Rule 3: Bounty Claims

    Must post screenshot/clip of death message to Admin ticket proving elimination. Lack of proof forfeits coin payout.

    Rule 4: Civilian Protection

    Assassins may only engage willing combatants/targets. Killing a civilian results in immediate Excommunicado.
